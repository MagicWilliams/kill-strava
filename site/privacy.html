Tempo Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Tempo is a running-coach app for iPhone, currently in a small TestFlight beta. It is built and run by one person, David Latimore II (Delight Studio), and its source code is public on GitHub, so anything this page says can be checked against the code.

The short version

  • No sign-up. Tempo never asks for your name, email or a password.
  • Your GPS routes and second-by-second health samples stay on your phone, in Apple Health.
  • Tempo's server stores run summaries, your plan, your journal entries and your coach chat, so that the coach can work.
  • Coach messages are processed by Anthropic's Claude.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics companies. Your data is never sold or shared with anyone else, and health data is never used for advertising.

Your account

On first launch Tempo creates an anonymous account: a random ID with no name, email or password attached. That ID is saved in your iPhone's Keychain and is how Tempo's server knows which records are yours.

Because nothing else identifies you, the account lives on your phone. If you delete the app or move to a new phone without restoring a backup, Tempo may not be able to reconnect you to your old records. Your runs themselves are unaffected, because they live in Apple Health.

What Tempo reads from Apple Health

With your permission, Tempo reads your workouts, heart rate, walking and running distance, step count, active energy, and workout routes. It uses them to show your runs, measure your fitness and training load, and adapt your plan.

When you record a run in Tempo, it saves the workout, its route, distance and active energy back to Apple Health. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

What stays on your phone

  • Location. Tempo uses your location only while a run you started is being recorded, to map the route and measure distance and pace. It stops when the run ends. The route is saved to Apple Health and is not uploaded to Tempo's server.
  • Motion. Step data from your phone's motion sensor is used to measure treadmill distance and, if you turn it on, to auto-pause. It isn't uploaded.
  • Raw health samples. Heart-rate and other sample-by-sample readings are read on your phone to calculate summaries; only the summaries below are uploaded.

What Tempo stores on its server

To coach you over time and keep your plan, Tempo stores:

  • Run summaries: date and time, distance, duration, pace, average heart rate, per-split pace and heart rate, and training-load figures calculated from them.
  • Training details you give it: goals and race dates, your answers to the training setup questions, your training plan, and optionally your birthdate or maximum heart rate (used to set heart-rate zones).
  • Run journal entries: the effort rating, feel, notes, title and shoe you add to a run, and any photo you attach. You choose each photo; Tempo doesn't otherwise access your camera or photo library.
  • Coach conversations: the messages you exchange with the coach.
  • Diagnostic events: short records of errors and retries inside the app (for example, "a sync failed"), with app and iOS version. These contain no health data and are used only to find and fix bugs.

This data is stored with Supabase, which hosts Tempo's database and files. It is encrypted in transit, and database access rules mean each account can read only its own records. Photos are kept in private storage, not at a public link.

The AI coach

When you message the coach, or Tempo builds or adjusts a plan, Tempo sends your message, your recent conversation, and a summary of your training (run summaries, plan, goals and journal notes) to Anthropic's Claude API to write the reply. Anthropic processes it to generate the response and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models.

Who else sees your data

Only the services named above, Supabase and Anthropic, and only to run the app. As the developer, David can access the database to fix problems. Tempo has no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share personal or health data with anyone. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing.

Apple separately collects TestFlight crash reports and any feedback you send through TestFlight, under Apple's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

Tempo keeps your data for as long as the beta runs, so your history and plan carry on. To have everything Tempo's server holds about you deleted, email david@delight.studio and it will be removed within 30 days. Because accounts are anonymous, include roughly when you started using Tempo, or send a screenshot of the You tab, so the right records can be found. Deleting Tempo's data doesn't touch anything in Apple Health.

Children

Tempo is not intended for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.
